## Introduction

Hey everyone! I'm sharing with you the music that makes up Volume 1 of the Official Original Soundtrack for Stardust Sandbox. Feel free to listen to them and share them with your friends!

All the music was composed by me independently, using a small open-source program called LMMS Studio. Although it doesn't offer real instruments natively, I was able to use high-quality soundfonts based on professional keyboards, which allowed me to create music with a very satisfying sound, even without having a real instrument in my hands.

I'm not a professional musician, but I did my best to create compositions capable of conveying the emotions and atmosphere of the game. Some ambient music artists, like Daniel Rosenfeld (better known as C418, composer of the Minecraft soundtrack), were great inspirations during this process. Much of my musical knowledge comes from piano lessons I've been taking recently, along with my passion for music in general.

I still intend to compose new music for the game and, who knows, start producing Volume 2 of the soundtrack. I like to imagine that each volume will represent a specific phase in both the development of Stardust Sandbox and my own musical evolution. Volume 1, for example, brings together music created during the first months of project development and reflects the learning I acquired during that time. When I feel that my skills have evolved enough to create even more engaging compositions, I believe that will be the ideal time to launch Volume 2.

Despite the difficulties encountered along the way, I'm very happy with the final result of this music and I hope you enjoy it too. If you want to share it, feel free to do so. And if you want to leave feedback, whether positive or negative, I'll be very happy to read it.
