# Official Steam Release

Hello, everyone! I'm thrilled to share some exciting news: Stardust Sandbox is now officially available on Steam! This is a special moment for the project and, honestly, very meaningful for me as well.

When it all started, there were no grand plans. It was just another experiment among many others I had done before. But you know how it goes? Life is full of surprises. Seeing this project grow, develop its own identity, and become something tangible is simply incredible.

If you'd like to learn more about Stardust Sandbox — its history, concepts, and much more — I recommend checking out the official launch announcement on Steam. There I share more details about everything:

**Special thanks** to everyone who followed this journey — from those who tested earlier versions and provided valuable feedback, to those who simply took time to explore this universe. I hope you have fun creating, exploring, and building your own stories in Stardust Sandbox.

Want to support the project? The best way is by purchasing the game on Steam. And if you're enjoying the experience, a positive review helps tremendously!

Thank you all. See you around!
