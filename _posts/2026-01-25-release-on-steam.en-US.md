# Release on Steam

Hello everyone, I hope you are all doing well!

I am very happy to announce some great news regarding the **Stardust Sandbox** project: the game will be officially released on the **Steam** platform!

As an independent developer, this represents an extremely significant and personal achievement. I never imagined that I would actually be able to get this far, but that moment has finally arrived.

**Stardust Sandbox** started as a small experimental project aimed at testing programming knowledge and practices. Over time, however, it evolved considerably, becoming a stable experience with several possibilities for players.

Being available on **Steam** is a major milestone that will open new doors for the game and allow it to continue evolving. Thank you very much to everyone who has supported the project since the beginning — this achievement is yours as well.

The official release on the platform will take place on **March 3rd, 2026**, but the Steam page is already available. Therefore, if you find the project interesting, I kindly ask you to consider adding it to your **Wishlist**.

This simple action is already a great help and directly contributes to spreading the project to even more people.
