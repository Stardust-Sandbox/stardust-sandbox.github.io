## 1. New Features

### 1.1. Journal System

One of the current development goals is to encourage more exploration and experimentation.

During some tests, we noticed many players tend to use only a small portion of the available elements, leaving several mechanics and interactions less visible during the experience.

To help with this, we are developing a journal system that will function like an in-game encyclopedia.

The journal will be filled gradually as the player discovers new content. For example, when a player uses an element for the first time, a new entry will be unlocked containing basic information about it. As new interactions and reactions are discovered, additional information will be recorded.

The goal is not to create a grind system, but to encourage curiosity and reward natural exploration.

### 1.2. New Elements and Entities

The development of new elements and entities continues to progress steadily.

Some content planned for the previous update was delayed due to prioritization of other systems, but it remains in development for future versions of the game.

Planned additions include:

* New types of vegetation
* Vines and flower species
* BGOs (Background Objects) to create more detailed scenes
* New creatures, such as fish and ants

These additions will help make environments feel more alive, varied, and visually rich.

## 2. Improvements

### 2.1. Overhaul of the Achievements System

The current achievements system serves its basic purpose but remains fairly simple.

We are working on a complete overhaul to make it more engaging and progressive.

The plan is to introduce dependency relationships between achievements, forming something like a progression tree. This should encourage more exploration, experimentation, and discovery of mechanics throughout the player's journey.

### 2.2. Fixes, Optimizations, and Restructuring

Much of the development also happens behind the scenes.

Even when there are no major visible content additions, the project continues to receive internal improvements, including:

* Bug fixes
* Performance optimizations
* System refactoring
* Project architecture organization
* Codebase improvements

These efforts are essential to ensure stability, performance, and to facilitate future updates.

As Stardust Sandbox approaches its third year of development, maintaining and evolving the project's structure becomes increasingly important.
