Welcome to the **Stardust Sandbox** project!

## Introduction

Stardust Sandbox is a creative sandbox simulation inspired by classic falling-sand games. It offers a free, experimental environment where you can explore physics-based interactions, build structures, trigger reactions, and observe emergent behavior in a world entirely under your control.

There are no predefined objectives or restrictions. The experience is nonlinear and driven entirely by curiosity, experimentation, and creativity. Whether you want to create complex systems, simple scenarios, or simply relax while watching elements interact, Stardust Sandbox adapts to your play style.

The game features a wide variety of interactive elements, each with unique properties and behaviors. Combining them leads to unexpected results, making every experiment feel different. Time manipulation tools let you pause, resume, and carefully observe every reaction in detail.

The controls are simple and intuitive. The mouse is used for most interactions, including placing elements and navigating menus, while the keyboard lets you move around larger maps and control the flow of time.

## Features

- 91 interactive elements with unique behaviors and interactions;
- Physics-based sandbox simulation;
- Time control: pause and resume the simulation at any time;
- Nonlinear, open-ended gameplay;
- Retro-style pixel art visuals;
- Original soundtrack;
- Day-and-night cycle system;
- Customizable interface elements.
