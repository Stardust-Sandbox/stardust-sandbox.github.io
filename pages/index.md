## Introduction

Welcome to the official **Stardust Sandbox** website. This is the entry point for the project's different editions and includes the main information you need to find the version you want to explore.

Before continuing, choose one of the available editions and then select your preferred language to access its content.

## About the Project

**Stardust Sandbox** is a creative *sandbox* simulation inspired by classic falling-sand games. The project offers a free and experimental environment where you can explore physics-based interactions, build structures, trigger reactions, and observe emergent behaviors in a world under your control.

There are no predefined objectives or restrictions. The experience is nonlinear and guided primarily by curiosity, experimentation, and creativity. You can create complex systems, build simple scenes, or simply observe the elements interacting.

The goal is to provide the freedom to experiment and discover different possibilities without requiring a specific way to play.

## Editions

The project has different editions, developed at different times and using different technologies. Select the edition you would like to learn more about below.

### [Legacy Edition](./legacy/index.html)

The **Legacy Edition** is the project's first version. It was developed entirely in *C#* using the **MonoGame** framework, the spiritual successor to the former *XNA*.

Development began on **August 5, 2023**, and the first playable version, identified as **1.0.0.0**, was released on **January 14, 2025**.

Although occasional updates are still planned, this edition will gradually be replaced by the Modern Edition and will eventually cease to be the project's primary development branch.

### [Modern Edition](./modern/index.html)

The **Modern Edition** is a complete reimplementation of the original project. This version uses C++17 and version 3 of the *Simple DirectMedia Layer* (SDL) library.

Development began on **September 14, 2026**, and the project is currently in its early stages. There is no estimated release date for the first playable version yet.
